# Style your menu

## The simple way

1. Open your menu and click **Design**.
2. Click a style.
3. Click **Save changes**.

<!-- screenshot: 15-design-presets.png -->

That is all most people need. Each style sets colors that are made to work together.

| Style | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| **Clean** | A light, neutral look. This is the default. |
| **Dark** | Sites with a dark header. |
| **Soft** | A gentle look with soft colors. |
| **Bold** | A strong look that stands out. |
| **Minimal** | A quiet look with little decoration. |

The preview changes as soon as you click. Try them all. Nothing goes on your site until you save.

You can also pick where the links sit in the bar. Use **Nav link alignment** to choose **Left**, **Center** or **Right**. The default is **Center**.

## Match your site's font

New menus use your site's font. An older menu may still use its own font. To match the rest of your site:

1. Open **Advanced settings**, then **Fonts**.
2. Set **Font** to **Same as my site**.
3. Click **Save changes**.

## Add your logo

1. Click **Content** and find **Logo**.
2. Turn on **Show logo**.
3. Click the picture button to choose your logo.
4. Fill in **Logo alternative text**. Screen readers read this out for people who can't see the image.

<!-- screenshot: 14-logo-panel.png -->

An SVG file looks sharpest. A PNG file with a clear background also works well.

## If a color warning appears

The plugin warns you if text may be hard to read on its background. The warning does not stop you from saving. It's best to change one of the two colors.

<!-- screenshot: 18-contrast-warning.png -->

## Advanced settings

Everything below is optional. It sits inside **Advanced settings**, which is closed until you open it.

<!-- screenshot: 16-advanced-collapsed.png -->

When the section is closed, you can still see what you changed. A short note names each setting that isn't at its default. Click a name to go right to it. So a hidden setting can't catch you by surprise.

<!-- screenshot: 17-advanced-open.png -->

Picking a style replaces the colors. If you edit a color, the style changes to **Custom**. If you then pick another style, **Undo** or the **Custom** card brings your colors back, until you reload the page. Once you save a new style and reload, use [History](undo-and-history.md) instead.

### Top menu item colors

- **Menu link color.** The color of the words in the menu bar.
- **Menu hover / open color.** The color when someone points at an item or opens it.

### Panel and brand colors

- **Header background.** The color behind the menu bar.
- **Category bar background.** The color behind the list of categories.
- **Active category background.** The color behind the category that is open.
- **Panel background.** The color of the panel that drops down.
- **Accent / button color.** The main color of your button and highlights.
- **Button text color.** The color of the words on your button.
- **Panel text color.** The color of the main words in the panel.
- **Muted / description color.** A softer color for short descriptions.
- **Border color.** The color of thin lines in the menu.

The chosen style sets the default for each color.

### Icon styling

- **Inherit menu text color.** Icons match the text color. Default: on.
- **Default icon color.** Pick your own icon color. Turn off the setting above first. Default: dark gray.
- **Hover / focus color.** The icon color when someone points at a link. Default: blue.
- **Current / active color.** The icon color for the page someone is on. Default: blue.
- **Icon size.** From 12 to 64 pixels. Default: 22.
- **Icon background.** A color behind each icon. Default: none.
- **Icon border color.** A line around each icon. Default: none.
- **Icon corner radius.** How round the icon's corners are. From 0 to 24. Default: 0, which is square.

These colors work on SVG icons. PNG, JPG and WebP pictures keep their own colors. They still use the size, background, border and corners.

To change one icon only, click that icon. Then use **Icon style for this item**.

### Layout and style

- **Full width mega menu.** The panel fills the width of the page. Turn it off to set your own width. Default: on.
- **Panel width.** Used when full width is off. From 640 to 1400 pixels. Default: 1000.
- **Category bar width.** How wide the list of categories is. From 180 to 420 pixels. Default: 280.
- **Category bar position.** Put the categories on the left, on the right or along the top. Default: left.
- **Default grid columns.** Used by a dropdown that hasn't set its own columns. Each dropdown can change this. Default: 3.
- **Corner radius.** How round the panel's corners are. From 0 to 24 pixels. Default: 8.
- **Shadow.** The shadow under the panel. Choose **None**, **Soft**, **Medium** or **Strong**. Default: **Medium**.
- **Button shape.** Choose **Square**, **Rounded** or **Pill**. Default: **Rounded**.
- **Panel title alignment.** Put panel titles on the left or in the center. Default: left.
- **Uppercase category titles.** Shows category titles in capital letters. Default: off.
- **Show category descriptions.** Shows the short line under each category. Default: on.
- **Transparent header surface.** Removes the bar's background so the page shows through. Use it when the menu sits on top of a large picture. Default: off.
- **Header stacking order.** Decides which menu sits on top when two overlap. Higher numbers go on top. Give a second menu a lower number than your main menu, so the main menu opens over it. Default: 1000.

### Fonts

- **Font.** Choose **Menu default**, **Same as my site** or **Custom font**. New menus start on **Same as my site**. Older menus keep **Menu default**.
- **Custom font names.** Type a list such as `Arial, sans-serif`. Your site must already load the font.
- **Size and weight.** Set these for the menu bar, category titles, links and descriptions. Weight means how thick the letters are. Leave a size empty to keep the default.
- **Menu bar letter spacing.** Adds space between letters.
- **Menu bar letter case.** Choose **As typed**, **UPPERCASE**, **Capitalize Each Word** or **lowercase**.

Sizes and weights change the desktop menu. The phone menu keeps its larger text, so it stays easy to tap.

Use **Reset fonts to default** to clear this group.

### Dropdown height

This changes how tall a panel can be on a computer.

- **Default.** The panel scrolls if it's too tall for the screen.
- **Grow to fit all links.** The panel never scrolls. On a short screen, it can run off the bottom.
- **Up to a height, then scroll.** The panel grows to a height you set, then scrolls.
- **Always the same height.** The panel stays one height.
- **Height.** Used by the last two choices. From 200 to 1200 pixels. Default: 520.

Use **Reset height to default** to clear this group.

### Mobile navigation

- **Enhanced mobile menu.** Adds a search box, rows that fill the screen and a Back button with a label to the phone menu. Default: off. See [Phone menus](phone-menus.md#enhanced-mobile-menu).
- **Phone menu button label.** Only for menus from Easy Mega Menu. See [Phone menus](phone-menus.md#phone-menu-button-label).

### Product Navigation

This shows only if you turned the feature on. It has a link to **Manage Product Navigation**. See the [user guide](user-guide.md#product-navigation).

### Logo details

These are on **Content**, in the **Logo** section. The last three sit under **More logo options**. That section opens by itself if one of them has been changed. A changed dark logo or height also shows in the note on **Design**.

- **Site title.** The words shown if you have no logo image. Default: your site's name.
- **Brand URL.** Where the logo goes when clicked. Default: your home page.
- **Dark-preset logo URL.** A second logo to use with the **Dark** style.
- **Logo height.** From 16 to 80 pixels. Default: 40.

## When your site's styles win

Your theme or a page builder can add its own style rules. A rule marked `!important` beats these settings. If a change doesn't show, that may be the reason. Ask whoever built your site to check.

Always check the final look on your site. The preview does not load your theme's styles.
