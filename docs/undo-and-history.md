# Undo mistakes

## The simple way

**Just made a mistake?** Click **Undo** at the top of the editor, or press **Ctrl+Z**. On a Mac, press **Cmd+Z**.

**Saved a mistake?** Go back to an earlier version:

1. Click **Mega Menu** in the sidebar.
2. Find your menu's card and click **⋯**.
3. Click **History**.
4. Pick a version and restore it.

You can also open **History** from the **⋯** menu in the editor.

<!-- screenshot: 32-history-versions.png -->

If you restore the wrong version, you can undo that too. Your menu keeps the version it had before.

## What each tool does

| You want to | Use | How far back it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fix what you just did | **Undo** | Your last 100 changes, until you leave the page |
| Go back to an earlier save of one menu | **History**, under **One menu** | Your last 10 versions of that menu |
| Undo an import or restore all your menus | **History**, under **All menus** | Your last 5 backups |
| Keep your own copy, or move menus to another site | **Export** | As long as you keep the file |
| Take a menu off your site for now | **Archive** | Until you delete it |

## Undo and redo

**Undo** and **Redo** sit at the top of the editor. They step through changes to your content and design.

- **Undo:** Ctrl+Z, or Cmd+Z on a Mac.
- **Redo:** Ctrl+Shift+Z, Cmd+Shift+Z on a Mac, or Ctrl+Y on Windows.

Inside a text box, these keys undo your typing as usual.

Undo starts when you open the editor. It ends when you leave.

## History for one menu

Each time you save, the plugin keeps a copy of your menu. It keeps your last 10 copies of each menu. If you save many times within five minutes, those saves count as one copy.

A menu's History is removed when you permanently delete that menu. All History is removed when you delete the plugin.

## Backups of all menus

The plugin saves a backup of all your menus before big changes, such as an import. It keeps the last five. These are in **History**, under **All menus**. **History** sits in **Tools** on the main screen.

<!-- screenshot: 33-history-backups.png -->

**Restoring a backup replaces all your menus,** not just one. So it's for big mistakes. The plugin saves your current menus as a new backup first.

Before you restore one, check its date and how many menus it has. These are backups of your menus only. They don't back up your whole site.

## Export a copy

Exporting saves your menus as a file on your computer. A file like this is called JSON.

- For one menu, click **⋯** on its card, then **Export**.
- From the editor, click **⋯**, then **Export as JSON**.

The file can hold private words, links and image addresses. So keep it somewhere safe, and don't post it in public.

An export is not a full backup. It doesn't include your products, branding or theme files.

## Import a file

Go to **Tools** on the main screen, then **Import and export**. Choose a file and a mode. You will see a preview before anything changes.

<!-- screenshot: 38-import-export.png -->

- **Copy** adds new menus and leaves your current menus alone. This is the safest choice.
- **Merge** adds new menus and updates ones with the same ID. Menus already on your site may change.
- **Replace** clears all your menus first. Menus not in the file are gone, and those spots on your site may go empty.

A file can be up to 10 MB, with 500 menus at most and 1 MB for each menu.

## Archive a menu

Archive takes a menu out of use but keeps it.

1. Click **⋯** on the menu's card, then **Archive**.
2. Read the message. It tells you how many places use this menu.

<!-- screenshot: 36-archive-dialog.png -->

**If the menu is placed, it will disappear from those pages.** Archive only if that's what you want. The plugin can't see inside theme files, so a count of zero doesn't always mean the menu is unused.

To bring it back, find it under **Archived menus** and click **Restore**. It keeps its ID, so it goes back to the same places.

<!-- screenshot: 37-archived-list.png -->

## Permanently delete

**Permanently delete** is in **Archived menus**. It can't be undone from that screen.

To stop accidents, you have to confirm with a second click. A quick double-click doesn't count, and the request resets after five seconds. The plugin also saves a backup first.

## If you leave with unsaved changes

The plugin warns you. You can choose one of these:

- **Save and leave** saves your work.
- **Leave, keep a copy** keeps your work in this browser only.
- **Keep editing** stays on the page.

<!-- screenshot: 35-exit-dialog.png -->

## Getting unsaved changes back

Your browser keeps a copy of work you haven't saved. It's not on your site, and other people can't see it.

If you come back to a menu with unsaved changes, you will see when they were made and three choices:

- **Restore my changes** brings your work back.
- **Download a copy** saves it as a file.
- **Discard my changes** throws it away.

<!-- screenshot: 34-unsaved-changes.png -->

The copy stays in that one browser. It won't show up on another computer.

Sometimes the saved menu is newer than your copy. You can still look at your changes, but you can't save them over the newer menu. This protects the newer work. Download your copy, then make the changes you want in the saved menu.

## If someone else saved first

If two people edit the same menu, the second person's save stops with a warning. This protects the first person's work. Keep your page open, reload the menu in a new tab, and make your changes again.

If a save fails, keep the page open and press **Retry**. Your changes are also kept in your browser.
